. data trustees are responsible for:
• the establishment of administrative data management policies and procedures
• approving all requests, whether new or changes, for agreements/partnerships involving sharing
university data from their data domain with third party vendors and ensuring these requests
follow the university's official it governance processes for review and approval
• assigning data management accountability to appropriate data stewards.
data steward – typically a senior operational manager in a functional area responsible for a data
stewardship domain.
data stewards are responsible for all of the following for data within their data
stewardship domain:
• developing and documenting data standards
• documenting and annually updating data integrations and partnering with information services to
map third party data sources and destinations (effective may 1, 2024)
• establishing the business rules and business processes for their respective data stewardship
domains
