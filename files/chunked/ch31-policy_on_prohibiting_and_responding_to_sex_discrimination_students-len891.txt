. consolidation of complaints.
when the allegations of sex discrimination arise out of the same facts
or circumstances, the title ix coordinator may consolidate two or more complaints of sex
discrimination: (1) against the same respondent(s); (2) against multiple respondents; or (3) when the
complaint and respondent initiate complaints against each other.
