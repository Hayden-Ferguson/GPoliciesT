. hrm-4004.3 — reimbursement of employee expenses
employees with approved out-of-state work arrangements are expected to maintain an appropriate office
environment suitable for the conduct of university business.
employees may be reimbursed for the ordinary
and necessary business expenditures incurred within the employee's scope of employment and directly related
to services performed for the university.
