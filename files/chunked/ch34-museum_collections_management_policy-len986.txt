acd-8101.16 - incoming loans
incoming loans may be accepted from an individual or organization for the purpose of exhibition, research, or
educational programming.
the executive director has the sole authority to approve loans.
the museums
reserve the right to determine when, if, and, for how long objects borrowed will be exhibited.
loan periods are typically restricted to a maximum two-year duration, possibly longer if extended to the
needed length of time for traveling exhibition tours organized by the museums.
after careful review, and with
special justification, the executive director may approve a loan renewal.
special circumstances for renewal
would include if the object were integral to an exhibition, an active educational program, or for research.
permanent loans will not be accepted.
long-term loans will not be accepted from individuals but may be
allowable from other museums or collecting organizations.
