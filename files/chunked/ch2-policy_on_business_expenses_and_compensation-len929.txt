fin-4102 – policy on business expenses and compensation
university of richmond | 2
appropriate
appropriate means an expense is suitable or fitting in the eyes of an objective third party and fulfills a valid
business purpose.
business purpose
the business purpose establishes that a particular good, service or activity is necessary to fulfill the mission
of the university and primarily benefits the university.
necessary
an expense is necessary when it is required to achieve a particular business purpose.
reasonable expense
a reasonable expense is ordinary – not lavish or excessive – and reflects a prudent decision to incur
the expense on behalf of university in furtherance of its mission.
