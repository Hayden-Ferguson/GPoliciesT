. investigation of complaints of discrimination
1. assignment of investigator.
upon receipt of a complaint and provided that the complainant and
respondent have not agreed to an alternative resolution process, the title ix coordinator shall conduct
the investigation.
the investigation will be conducted in a prompt, objective, and thorough manner.
2. opportunity to present witnesses and evidence.
the investigator shall provide the complainant and
the respondent with an equal opportunity to notify the investigator of fact witnesses and to provide
inculpatory and exculpatory information to the investigator that is relevant and not impermissible under
this policy. the investigator shall contact the fact witnesses identified by the complainant and
respondent and shall interview all relevant witnesses who agree to participate in the investigation.
