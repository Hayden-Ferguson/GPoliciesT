. hrm-2008.3 – grandfathered employees
police officers hired prior to july 1, 2012, who work twelve (12) hour shifts may continue to schedule
their university holidays throughout the year, with approval from their supervisor, at the rate of eight
hours (8) per university holiday for a total of 120 hours per year.
this group of employees will
continue to be paid only regular time for any holidays worked.
holiday hours must be used in half day
increments and used within each calendar year and cannot be carried over.
