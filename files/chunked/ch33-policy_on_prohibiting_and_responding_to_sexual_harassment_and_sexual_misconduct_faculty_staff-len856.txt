. course of conduct. a course of conduct means two or more acts, including, but not limited to,
acts in which the person directly, indirectly, or through third parties, by any action, method,
device, or means, follows, monitors, observes, surveils, threatens, or communicates to or about,
another person, or interferes with another person's property.
b. substantial emotional distress.
substantial emotional distress means significant mental
suffering or anguish that may, but does not necessarily, require medical or other professional
treatment or counseling.
q. sexual violence.
the term "sexual violence" means any physical sexual act or acts perpetrated against a
person's will or against a person incapable of giving consent.
examples of sexual violence include non-
consensual sexual contact, non-consensual sexual intercourse, and sexual assault.
