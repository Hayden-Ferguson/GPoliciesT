acd-1605.1 – definitions
academic programs include degrees (including an undergraduate or graduate certificate) as well as majors,
minors, or concentrations (if the concentration is recorded on a student's transcript).
degree: a degree is the certification that is awarded by the university upon completion of a specific course
of study.
