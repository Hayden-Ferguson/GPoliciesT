imposing restrictions
the president, by proclamation, may impose restrictions to provide for the protection of life, property, and the
environment.
the president may impose as many restrictions as reasonably necessary to maintain an
acceptable level of public order and services and to protect lives, safety, and property.
the president should
detail in writing his or her reasons for imposing restrictions in the proclamation.
