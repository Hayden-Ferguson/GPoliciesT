stu-2001.2 process
the required documentation must be submitted by july 15 for fall admission and january 15 for spring
admission for review by the student health center staff.
any student with insufficient records will be
contacted via a secure message in their patient portal with specific instructions for satisfactorily completing
the process.
any student with incomplete records after three weeks of classes will have a hold applied to their student
account to block registration for next semester classes.
the hold will remain in place until all record
requirements are met.
