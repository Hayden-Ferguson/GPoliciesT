de minimis
a non-cash gift, prize, or award that is so small and infrequent that accounting for it would be unreasonable or
administratively impractical is considered a de minimis benefit by the internal revenue service (irs).
per
irs regulations, cash, gift cards, and gift certificates are never considered de minimis.
