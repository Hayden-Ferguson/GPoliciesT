. responsible use of university information and computing resources
all members of the university of richmond community who use the university's computing and network
facilities must use them in an ethical, responsible, and legal manner.
this means that individuals are
personally responsible for their use of these resources.
individuals must be familiar with and follow all
university and information services policies.
an attempt to engage in a prohibited activity is considered a
violation whether the attempt is successful or not.
