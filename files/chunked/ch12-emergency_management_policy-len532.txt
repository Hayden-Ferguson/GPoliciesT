sec-2003.5 – building emergency plans
each academic or administrative building on campus shall complete and update annually a building
emergency plan (bep).
to accomplish this task:
a) each building shall designate a primary and secondary point of contact for emergency coordination.
any
changes to emergency coordination points of contact should be reported to the office of emergency
management as soon as possible.
b) beps shall adhere to the format provided by the office of emergency management
