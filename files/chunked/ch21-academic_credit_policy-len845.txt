. academic credit for independent research paper
students may receive one, two, or three-credits for preparing an independent research (ir)
paper.
to earn one credit, the student must prepare a paper of at least 2,500 words; to earn
two credits the student must prepare a paper of at least 5,000 words; and to earn three
credits, the student must prepare a paper of at least 7,500 words.
all word counts are
exclusive of footnotes.
in addition to meeting the word-court requirements, students must
spend at least 42.5 hours per ir credit-hour researching their topic, writing and editing their
paper, or meeting with their faculty advisor.
the upper-level writing requirement (ulwr) necessitates a paper of at least 5,000
words, exclusive of footnotes.
thus, to receive ulwr credit, the student must enroll in at
least 2 credits of ir coursework
