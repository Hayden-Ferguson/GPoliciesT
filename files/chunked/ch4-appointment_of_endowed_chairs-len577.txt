. acd-7301.2 – appointment of endowed faculty chairs
appointments to endowed faculty chairs should reflect the highest ideals of academic excellence and
scholarly or creative achievement. appointments to endowed chairs must comply with the terms of the
gift documentation, applicable university and regulatory policies, and relevant sections of the faculty
handbook. except in the rare case of visitors appointed to endowed chairs, appointments to faculty
endowed chairs are made by the board of trustees upon recommendation of the provost and president. terms of appointment
