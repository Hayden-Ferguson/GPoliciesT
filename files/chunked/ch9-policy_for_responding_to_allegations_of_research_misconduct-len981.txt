this policy replaces the previous
research misconduct policy of the university of richmond, effective january 31, 2011.
under this policy, the executive vice president & provost will serve as or designate the "deciding official"
for allegations of research misconduct.
the provost will also designate the research integrity officer (rio),
who also serves as the university director of compliance and title ix coordinator.
when a member of the university community has reason to believe that research misconduct has occurred, he
or she is obligated to report such concerns to the rio.
a potential complainant may also seek guidance from
the rio as to whether or not such concerns warrant a complaint
