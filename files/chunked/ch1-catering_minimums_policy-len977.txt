policy manual
purpose:
the implementation of a minimum order policy by university catering is designed to maximize resources,
curate diverse and high-quality menus, and deliver the best possible service for all university events.
scope:
this policy applies to anyone who requests university of richmond catering services, both internal customers
as well as external customers.
