fin-3102.1 – secondary positions and additional compensation
employees who have a secondary position that includes additions to base pay, including full-time
administrative appointments with an appointment letter (such as associate dean, associate provost) and staff
with interim pay must include fringe allocation on the secondary position at the full-time fringe benefit rate.
all other additional compensation for employees, including bonus, overload and summer teaching,
mentoring stipends, award payments, etc.
should include fringe allocation at the part-time rate
.
