gov-3103.2 – definitions
a. campus.
the term "campus" includes any building or property owned or controlled by the university
within the same reasonably contiguous geographic area of the university and used by the university in
direct support of, or in a manner related to, its educational purposes, including residence halls and
apartments and any building or property within or reasonably contiguous to the campus that is owned
by the university, is frequently used by students, and supports university purposes.
