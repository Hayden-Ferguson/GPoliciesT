policy sponsor
official from responsible university office who is responsible for developing or revising policy and
seeing process through to completion of approved policy.
responsibilities include:
• drafting
• identifying key stakeholders, obtaining and considering input
• working with appropriate university officials to ensure conformity with applicable laws and
regulations
• ensuring conformity with existing university policies
