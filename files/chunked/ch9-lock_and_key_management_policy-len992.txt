emergency entry
individuals who do not possess university keys or card access and must gain emergency entry to a
university building or area may request assistance from the university of richmond police department
(urpd).
upon the receipt of such request, urpd must verify that the requestor has authorization for
access and secure permission from the appropriate official, if not already handled by prior arrangement,
to allow the requestor to enter the designated area and record the action taken in the daily
communications log.
