e. consolidation of formal complaints.
when the allegations of sexual harassment/sexual misconduct arise
out of the same facts or circumstances, the title ix coordinator may consolidate two or more formal
complaints of sexual harassment/sexual misconduct: (1) against the same respondent(s); (2) against
multiple respondents; or (3) when the complainant and respondent initiate formal complaints against each
other.
