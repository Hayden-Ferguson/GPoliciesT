intercollegiate athletics alcohol & drug policy
the university of richmond athletic department has additional written policies that are
presented to each student-athlete annually prior to participation.
these policies encompass
mandatory drug testing, sanctions as a result of positive drug tests, educational programs relative
to drug and alcohol use, misuse and counseling.

stu-1002 – alcohol, tobacco and other drug policy
drug free workplace policy
the university is a drug-free workplace.
the university does not tolerate the unlawful manufacture,
dispensing, possession, use, or distribution of illegal drugs, marijuana and/or alcohol by employees
on the university's property, or as part of its activities, except in compliance with this and related
policies.
